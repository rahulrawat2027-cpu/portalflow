# Competitor subscription and payment research: agency portals

Checked 16 October 2026 using the actual official pages below. USD figures are advertised prices, not an account-specific checkout quote. Taxes, geography, provider eligibility and negotiated offers may affect the final amount. Annual totals explicitly marked calculated are arithmetic, not observed checkout totals. Search snippets were not treated as authoritative when opened pages differed.

## ManyRequests

| Plan | Monthly billing | Annual billing advertised monthly equivalent | Annual base total, calculated | Included team seats |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Core | $59 | $39 | $468 | 1 |
| Pro | $99 | $79 | $948 | 1 |
| Enterprise | Starts at $1,000/month | Contact sales | Not verified | Custom |

Extra team seats: Core $20/month or $15/month on annual billing; Pro $30/month or $25/month annually. Invited clients do not consume team seats. Both published standard tiers allow unlimited clients. The page offers a 14-day trial without a card, no onboarding fee and cancellation anytime. Its blanket “Save 20%” label does not match Core's $59-to-$39 arithmetic; use exact prices rather than repeating the percentage. No first-month percentage coupon was verified. Sources: [pricing](https://manyrequests.com/pricing), [seat billing](https://help.manyrequests.com/en/articles/12050448-understanding-seat-pricing).

Own SaaS subscription: the seat-billing help page refers to the customer's credit card failing; a complete accepted card-brand/wallet list was not verified. Agency collection: pricing names Stripe and says ManyRequests adds no transaction fee, leaving Stripe fees. Help documents credit-card checkout and manual transfers with pending invoices requiring payment confirmation. These are agency-to-client collection options, not proof of methods accepted for buying ManyRequests itself. [Client checkout](https://help.manyrequests.com/en/articles/9531636-how-can-my-clients-submit-requests).

## SuiteDash

| Plan | Monthly billing | Explicit annual total | Calculated annual monthly equivalent |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Start | $19 | $180 | $15 |
| Thrive | $49 | $480 | $40 |
| Pinnacle | $99 | $960 | $80 |

All tiers advertise unlimited clients and staff. Standard introductory offer: 14-day Pinnacle trial without a card. Thrive includes one onboarding session; Pinnacle adds a bonus session. The page also displays “Century” purchases: $2,240/$3,940/$6,840 respectively; these are not annual prices. [Official pricing](https://suitedash.com/pricing/).

Separate SuiteDreams package: $1,499 upfront plus $49/month while active, 21-day no-card trial and 30-day post-upgrade money-back guarantee. The support page advertises a 500-package limit; remaining inventory was not verified. Do not present this as a universally available first-month discount. [Package terms](https://help.suitedash.com/article/613-suitedreams-package).

Own subscription: credit card is documented as the primary payment method charged for monthly/yearly plans and add-ons; complete brands and alternative methods were not verified. [Primary payment method](https://help.suitedash.com/article/282-changing-your-primary-payment-method).

Agency collection: Stripe, Stripe ACH, Braintree, Authorize.net and PayPal are documented gateways. SuiteDash's direct PayPal gateway supports one-time invoices, not subscription payments; Stripe, Braintree and Authorize.net support subscriptions. Availability depends on configured gateway and eligibility. SuiteDash states it takes no cut of client payments. [Gateway documentation](https://help.suitedash.com/article/89-invoices-payment-gateways).

## Plutio

| Plan | Monthly billing | Published annual approximation | Active clients | Contributors |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- | --- |
| Core | $19 | About $15/month | 9 | Solo only |
| Pro | $49 | About $39/month | Unlimited | 30 included |
| Max | $199 | About $159/month | Unlimited | Unlimited, fair-use terms |

The pricing page markets annual billing as two months free but gives approximate equivalents, not exact annual totals; annual checkout totals were not verified. It offers seven days without a card, with limited access and data export if unpaid afterward. No first-month percentage coupon was verified. It says major credit/debit cards through Stripe and PayPal are accepted for its own subscription; the [billing FAQ](https://help.plutio.com/help/faq-billing) only confirms Visa, Mastercard and American Express through Stripe. Treat PayPal subscription availability as unconfirmed until checkout. [Pricing](https://www.plutio.com/pricing).

Plan limits are confirmed by [official plan comparison](https://www.plutio.com/help/subscription-plans-overview); that page limits trial capacity to three active clients and three contributors. An opened refund-policy page inconsistently mentioned eight trial days; seven days is supported by the pricing, current plan comparison and terms. Do not promise an eight-day trial.

Agency collection is separate: [invoicing product page](https://www.plutio.com/product/invoicing) confirms Stripe, Square and PayPal. [Manual bank-transfer documentation](https://help.plutio.com/help/online-payments-bank-transfer) distinguishes manually verified transfers from automated US-bank ACH through Stripe on USD invoices. No assumption should be made that each agency collection method can buy Plutio's own subscription.

## Product implications for PortalFlow (recommendations, not competitor facts)

- Use a clearly timed no-card trial, monthly/annual selector, exact annual charge and visible renewal price. Do not invent a competitor's first-month coupon.
- A new venture should test one entry plan before copying mature vendors' broad tier promises. A proposed introductory discount must be described as PortalFlow's offer, with duration, eligibility and undiscounted renewal shown before purchase.
- Client access can be included while paid agency seats or finite capacity control costs. Do not advertise unlimited storage or team seats unless implemented and supportable.
- Keep PortalFlow subscription checkout separate from agencies collecting client invoices. A provider logo is not evidence that an integration is connected, approved, tested or live.
- Avoid lifetime/century and perpetual founder discounts until real operating costs are measured. A free trial plus a bounded first-paid-period discount is easier to budget and explain.
