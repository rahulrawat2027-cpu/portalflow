# PortalFlow launch pricing — proposal, 16 October 2026

## Scope and status

The user confirmed India and then explicitly requested skipping payments. This update develops competitor research and the pricing experience only. It does not install a payment processor, create subscriptions, collect cards, start trial timers, restrict existing beta access, or change agency invoice payments. Published prices on the local draft are proposed commercial terms for review, not live checkout offers.

## Recommended first offer

Launch with one plan, **Launch**, for one agency account and its client workflow. Standard proposed price: **₹999/month**. Optional annual billing: **₹9,990 paid upfront for 12 months**, equivalent to **₹832.50/month**, saving **₹1,998** against twelve standard monthly payments (16.67%). Price amounts are INR; competitor USD amounts are not converted using an invented exchange rate.

Proposed introductory structure: **14-day trial with no card**, followed by an explicit paid opt-in. For first-time monthly subscribers only, the first paid month is **₹499**, followed by **₹999/month**. This is PortalFlow's proposed acquisition experiment; no competitor first-month percentage coupon was verified. It applies once per workspace, does not stack with the annual saving, and does not imply a lifetime price lock. Annual selection removes the introductory monthly offer. The proposed trial begins at the future paid launch; the current beta remains free and has no trial countdown or automatic charges.

The amount displayed must be the complete payable total once tax handling is established. The current preview is not a tax invoice and adds no invented tax rate. Tax treatment and final provider quote must be resolved before taking payments. Renewal amount, billing interval, cancellation behavior, applicable taxes and refund policy must be disclosed before accepting an actual subscription. No policy promises are made without an operational owner and way to honor them.

## Why one plan

ManyRequests and SuiteDash use 14-day no-card trials; Plutio uses seven days; annual discounts are common. Their feature scope, operations, storage and integrations exceed PortalFlow's current release. One affordable plan lets a new venture test willingness to pay while avoiding unsupported premium tiers, fake lifetime scarcity or indefinite discount liabilities. ₹999 is a proposed market test, not a researched optimal price or profitability guarantee. Validate with interviews and paid conversion once onboarding and hosting are ready.

Current supported value: shared agency/client access on the local server, requests/tasks, version decisions/comments and text comparison, client messages, manual invoices/time records, file snapshots and exports. One agency account is supported; granular staff roles, hosted custom domains, email delivery, payments, legal signatures and unlimited storage are not included. Files remain limited to 1 MiB; shared server content quotas apply. The page must not portray local backups as cloud disaster recovery or claim parity with mature vendors.

## Economics to measure before charging

Track hosting, database/backup storage, customer support, provider fees and taxes separately. At ₹999, gross standard monthly revenue for ten paying workspaces would be ₹9,990 before any costs; introductory first-month gross for ten would be ₹4,990. Ten first-month subscribers therefore require subsidy or sufficient margin to cover actual service costs. Measure activation (first genuine client invited and first decision), trial-to-paid conversion, monthly cancellations and support burden. Do not assume conversion rates, zero processing fees or recurring-payment approval.

A future integration must establish approved provider access, publicly reachable HTTPS backend, server-owned price identifiers and amounts, verified signed payment events with duplicate handling, workspace/account binding, one-use introductory eligibility, failed-payment/cancellation behavior, refunds and explicit customer mandate consent.
