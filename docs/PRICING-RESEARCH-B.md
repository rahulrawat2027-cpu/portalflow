# Pricing evidence: Assembly, Filestage, Wayfront

Checked 16 October 2026. USD list prices; annual totals below are calculated as displayed monthly equivalent × 12. Quotes, taxes, extra users, processor charges, and regional eligibility may change the payable total. This research did not create accounts or submit payments.

## Assembly

| Plan | Monthly billing | Annual monthly equivalent | Annual total, calculated |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Free | $0 | $0 | $0 |
| Starter | $49 | $29 | $348 |
| Professional | $139 | $99 | $1,188 |
| Advanced | $599 | $499 | $5,988 |
| Enterprise | Custom | Custom | Custom |

Official pricing was checked in both billing-switch states. Animated digits confuse text extraction, so the displayed numbers were visually inspected. Free permits 5 active contacts, 1 internal user, 75 build credits and 3 apps. Starter has 50 contacts/1 user; Professional 500/3; Advanced unlimited contacts/5. Current entry offer is free forever. No universal first-subscription coupon was verified. Annual discounts apply to the base plan; upgrades apply immediately, downgrades/cancellation at period end. [Official pricing](https://assembly.com/pricing).

**Payment distinction:** Assembly's own subscription terms require an authorized payment method and advance monthly/annual billing; the checked terms do not enumerate every supported method. Do not label its SaaS subscription checkout “Apple Pay/ACH supported” solely from the client-payment feature. [Terms](https://assembly.com/legal/terms-of-service).

Its agency-facing invoicing product supports credit/debit cards, ACH, and Apple Pay, plus recurring subscriptions and fee controls. These are agency-client collection methods. [Invoicing product](https://assembly.com/invoicing).

**Uncertainty:** Older official blogs still mention a 14-day trial. Current pricing explicitly promotes a permanent free plan; do not substitute the older trial claim for the current offer. A no-card free start is advertised on a current solution page. [Client onboarding](https://assembly.com/client-onboarding-system).

## Filestage

| Plan | Monthly billing | Annual monthly equivalent | Annual total, calculated |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Free | $0 | $0 | $0 |
| Starter | $249 | $199 | $2,388 |
| Business | $399 | $329 | $3,948 |
| Enterprise | Custom | Custom | Custom |

Official interactive pricing was checked with Yearly and Monthly selected. The 14-day no-card trial moves to Free unless upgraded. Free includes 1 active project, 5 new files/month, 10 team members, and unlimited reviewers. Paid tiers unlock unlimited projects/files; more seats come in five-user bundles. Annual pricing is advertised as two months free. The exact rounded displayed prices above take precedence over assuming a mathematically exact 10-month charge. Education/nonprofit discounts are custom, with no universal first-subscription coupon verified. [Official pricing](https://filestage.io/pricing/).

**Payment distinction:** Its own subscription defaults to credit card, with bank transfer available for enterprise contracts. Monthly/yearly contracts are paid upfront; enterprise annual terms can use Net 30. [Official purchasing/payment guide](https://help.filestage.io/en/articles/5000929-buy-a-subscription-conclude-a-license-agreement).

Filestage is a proofing competitor. Agency-client invoice collection/payment gateway support was not verified; do not infer it from its own SaaS billing options.

## SPP.co → Wayfront

SPP.co's pricing URL redirects to Wayfront; its official footer identifies Service Provider Pro as now Wayfront. Old SPP price tables should not be presented as current.

| Plan | Monthly billing | Annual monthly equivalent | Annual total, calculated |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Base | $129 | $99 | $1,188 |
| Pro | $299 | $249 | $2,988 |
| Plus | Custom | Custom | Custom |

Base includes 5 team seats; Pro 10. Pricing promotes unlimited clients/projects and no platform transaction fees. A 14-day trial requires no credit card; after trial the customer chooses a paid subscription using credit/debit card. No public universal first-subscription coupon was verified. Annual savings are the list-price difference, not a new-customer-only offer. Self-service cancellation is on the account billing page. [Official pricing](https://wayfront.com/pricing).

**Payment distinction:** Agency-client checkout connects Stripe or PayPal, supports recurring/one-off services, trials, setup fees, tax settings, downloadable invoices, and manual off-platform payment records. It offers coupon rules such as once per client, a total-use cap, or expiry. These are capabilities agencies can offer their clients, not evidence that Wayfront gives its own new subscribers a discount code. Its billing product also advertises a free onboarding call with the trial. [Billing features](https://wayfront.com/features/billing).

## Implications for PortalFlow — recommendations, not competitor facts

- Use a no-card 14-day trial with an explicit fallback instead of silently creating paid subscriptions. This follows verified competitor onboarding patterns while reducing risk for a new product.
- Show monthly price, annual total charged upfront, renewal amount, introductory duration, and cancellation effect together.
- Separate the platform subscription an agency pays PortalFlow from invoices an agency collects from clients. Each needs its own provider relationship and records.
- Avoid a permanent low-price or lifetime promise before storage, support and provider costs are measured. A proposed first-period discount should be explicitly PortalFlow's offer, with eligibility and renewal terms, rather than a claimed copied competitor coupon.
- Only advertise payment methods actually enabled by the approved provider account and customer region. Keep provider fees separate from PortalFlow's own fees.
